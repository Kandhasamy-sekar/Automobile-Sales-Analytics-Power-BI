# 🚗 Automobile Sales Analytics Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

The Automobile Sales Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze automobile sales performance, revenue, profitability, customer behavior, and regional sales patterns.

This project uses a 15,000-row automobile sales dataset and transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights through interactive dashboards and visualizations.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall automobile sales performance
- Track revenue and profit
- Compare brand-wise sales performance
- Analyze vehicle segments and vehicle types
- Understand fuel-type sales distribution
- Analyze customer behavior and ratings
- Identify regional sales patterns
- Provide executive-level business insights

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Automobile Sales Overview

Provides a high-level overview of automobile sales performance.

**Key Visuals:**

- Total Vehicles Sold
- Total Revenue
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Revenue by Brand
- Sales by Vehicle Type
- Sales by Region
- Top 10 Models

---

### 2. Vehicle & Product Performance

Focuses on brand and vehicle-level performance.

**Key Visuals:**

- Units Sold by Brand
- Revenue by Vehicle Segment
- Profit by Vehicle Type
- Sales by Fuel Type

---

### 3. Customer & Regional Insights

Analyzes customer behavior and regional sales patterns.

**Key Visuals:**

- Sales by Customer Type
- Sales by Region
- Sales by Customer Rating
- Sales by Customer Age Group
- Sales by Customer Gender

---

### 4. Executive Insights

Provides a summarized business-level view of automobile performance.

**Key KPIs:**

- Total Revenue
- Total Profit
- Average Profit Margin
- Average Customer Rating

**Key Visuals:**

- Revenue vs Profit by Year
- Profit by Brand
- Sales by Fuel Type
